Every page in its place.

Jiyda is a browser for Windows written in Rust. Instead of a row of tabs there are Spaces for each part of your life and Orbit, where you see everything at once. New Page picks up where you left off, and your history never leaves your computer.

Less browser, more web.

No tab strip shouting for attention. Pages, contexts and tools appear when you call them and step aside when you don’t.

Spaces

Separate contexts for work, study and home. Each Space keeps its own pages and its own cookies and logins.

Orbit

All your open pages, grouped by what they are about. Find a page in a second instead of hunting through tabs. Put a page aside in Later without closing it.

New Page that picks up the thread

Your last page, recently closed sites and your Spaces, right under the search. No wall of shortcuts: only what is already on your computer.

Split View

Two pages side by side in one window: read and write, compare, take notes.

Command palette

Ctrl+K opens pages, history, bookmarks and every command from one search field.

Protection built in

Known trackers and ads are blocked out of the box. Sites ask before using your camera, microphone or location.

Bookmarks at hand

A button on the toolbar opens folders and search, Ctrl+D saves the page. Bring bookmarks and history from Chrome or Edge in a minute.

Undo instead of “Are you sure?”

Closed a page or removed a bookmark by mistake? Press Undo. And after a crash your pages are still there.

Built in Rust.

Everything Jiyda does itself — Spaces, Orbit, protection, storing your history and bookmarks — is written in Rust, a language that rules out whole classes of memory errors. Pages are shown by Microsoft WebView2, the engine of Microsoft Edge.

No Electron

Jiyda doesn’t ship its own copy of Chromium. Its Rust core drives the WebView2 engine that is already in Windows.

About 4 MB

That is the whole installer: the page engine is usually already on Windows 10 and 11, so there is nothing heavy to download.

Engine kept up to date

Windows updates WebView2 automatically, the same way it updates Microsoft Edge, so security fixes for the engine reach Jiyda too.

Code in the open

Jiyda’s source code is public on GitHub: anyone can read how it handles your pages and your data.

Your browsing stays yours.

Jiyda has no account and no ads, and no analytics unless you turn them on. Your history, bookmarks and open pages are stored on your computer and nowhere else.

Jiyda does not collect

  • the pages you visit or your history
  • what you search for or type
  • page contents, cookies or passwords
  • crash reports, and usage statistics unless you turn on Help improve Jiyda (then only anonymous daily counts)

Jiyda connects only to

  • the websites you open and your search engine
  • GitHub, to check for and download updates (you can turn this off)
  • Anthropic, only if you set up Ask Jiyda with your own key
  • the Jiyda statistics server, only if you turn on Help improve Jiyda

Jiyda shows pages with Microsoft WebView2, the same engine Microsoft Edge uses. Read the privacy policy for what that means.

Made in Uzbekistan.

Jiyda is made in Uzbekistan and speaks Uzbek, Russian and English. It is free, and early: we are looking for the first people to try it and tell us what to fix.
