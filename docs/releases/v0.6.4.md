A new New Page that picks up where you left off, bookmarks one click away, and Jiyda now takes its colors from your Space.

## New

- **New Page that continues.** The row of site tiles is gone. Under the search there are now a few light cards built only from what's on your computer: *Continue where you left off* (the page you came from, and all open pages), *Recent* (pages you just closed and sites you visited), *Spaces*, and *Actions*. A card with nothing to show simply isn't there.
- **Bookmarks button.** A bookmark button now sits on the toolbar, between ✦ and ⋯ (Ctrl+Shift+O still works). It opens a panel on the right, over the page, without moving it: folders open in place, search finds bookmarks by name, address or folder, *Add current page* saves the page you're on, and each bookmark has a menu to open it, open it in a new page, edit, move or delete it. Click a bookmark to open it in the current page; Ctrl+click or the middle button opens it in the background.
- **Bookmark a page with Ctrl+D.** Ctrl+D (or ☆ in the address bar) opens a small window where you choose the name and folder; nothing is saved until you press *Save*. On a page that's already bookmarked, the same window lets you rename it, move it to another folder or remove it. A page is never bookmarked twice.
- **Colors follow your Space.** The search field on New Page glows softly in the color of the current Space, and buttons, switches, selections and focus rings use that color too. Change a Space's color and everything follows at once. The light theme uses a deeper shade so text stays readable, and Windows high contrast mode gets plain system colors without any glow.
- **Welcome tour.** On first start, Jiyda walks you through bringing over bookmarks from Chrome or Edge, picking a theme, protection and updates, and the keys that matter. Skip it at any time, or open it again from the command palette (*Welcome tour*).
- **Third-party licenses.** Notices for the open-source libraries Jiyda uses are in Settings → Advanced, in the installer, and on the website.

## Changes

- **Ctrl+D now bookmarks the page, like in other browsers.** Pinning a page moved to **Ctrl+Shift+D**. If you had changed either shortcut in Settings → Shortcuts, your choice stays.
- **New Page no longer animates its background,** so it doesn't keep redrawing while it's just sitting there.

## Install

Jiyda updates itself: wait for the **Update** banner, or run *Check for updates* from the command palette. You can also download `JiydaSetup-0.6.4.exe` below.
