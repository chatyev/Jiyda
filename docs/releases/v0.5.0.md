Seyro 0.5.0 is a new interface. Tabs and the sidebar are gone: while you read, the window is almost all page, and navigation appears when you need it.

## New

- **Orbit** (Ctrl+Space) replaces tabs. **Now** holds the pages you're using, **Later** the ones you've put aside, and **Archive** the closed ones and those you haven't opened in a while. Up to 12 pages are shown as cards with previews. With more, they're grouped by topic (Development, Documentation, Shopping…). **Context** at the top shows how you got to the current page. Drag a card to the edge of the window to open it in Split View.
- **Top bar.** Your Space on the left, the address in the center and the Windows caption buttons on the right, with Snap Layouts when you hover Maximize. Secondary buttons fade while you read.
- **Edge Reveal.** Move the pointer to the left edge of the window for a small panel: Orbit, Search, Spaces, History, Downloads, Later and Settings.
- **Spaces.** Each Space has its own pages, cookies and logins. Switch with Ctrl+Shift+Space, or jump straight to one with Ctrl+Shift+1…9. A page can be moved to another Space.
- **Command palette** (Ctrl+K) searches pages, history, commands and Spaces.
- **New Page** (Ctrl+T) shows search, your most visited sites, the time and today's protection summary.
- **Tracker and ad blocking** is built in. The shield in the address bar shows what was blocked on the page, and you can turn protection off for a site.
- **Site permissions** (camera, microphone, location, notifications…) are asked right under the address: *Allow once*, *Always allow* or *Block*. You can review and revoke them in Settings.
- **Ask Seyro** brings Claude by Anthropic, using your own API key, into the browser. It can summarize a page, explain or translate a selection, compare pages and help find something you've seen. Page text is sent only when you ask, and nothing at all without a key. The key is stored in Windows Credential Manager.
- **Selected text** gets a small toolbar: Copy · Search · Explain · Translate · Ask Seyro. You can turn it off in Settings.
- **Peek.** Shift+click a link to preview it over the current page. Close the preview and nothing is added.
- **Split View** (Ctrl+Shift+S), **Focus Mode** (Ctrl+Shift+F), **Downloads** (Ctrl+J), **History** (Ctrl+H) and **Recently closed** (Ctrl+Shift+H) have all been redesigned.
- **Undo instead of confirmations**, for example "Page closed · Undo".
- **Every keyboard shortcut can be changed** in Settings → Keyboard shortcuts.
- **Light and dark themes** follow Windows by default. Windows high contrast and reduced motion are respected. The interface is in English or Russian, matching Windows.

## Accessibility

- Screen readers announce the highlighted item in the command palette and in Orbit.
- Popovers and panels can be fully operated from the keyboard, and Esc closes them.

## Install

Seyro 0.4.0 updates itself: wait for the **Update** banner, or run *Check for updates* from the command palette. You can also download `SeyroSetup-0.5.0.exe` below and run it. Your pages, Spaces and logins are kept. Your old tabs appear in Orbit.

## Known limitations

- Site icons saved while a site was in dark mode can be hard to see in the light theme.
- Ask Seyro needs your own Anthropic API key.
